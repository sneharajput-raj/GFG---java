<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/maximum-area-rectangle/1?page=1&category=Java-Class%20and%20Object&sortBy=submissions">Maximum Area Rectangle</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Easy</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given a 2D array <strong>arr[][]</strong> of size n × 2, where arr[i] = [L, B] represents the length and breadth of the i-th rectangle, respectively.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Determine and return the <strong>maximum</strong> area among all the rectangles.</span></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">Examples:</span></strong></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>arr[] = [[1, 2], [3, 4], [5, 6]]</span>
<span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Output: </strong>30</span>
<span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Explanation:
</strong>Total area of Rect. 1 = 1 * 2 = 2
Total area of Rect. 2 = 3 * 4 = 12
Total area of Rect. 3 = 5 * 6 = 30
Out of all rectangles, Rectangle 3 has the maximum area.
</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>arr[] = [[12, 3], [40, 5]] </span>
<span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Output: </strong>200</span>
<span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Explanation:
</strong>Total area of Rect. 1 = 12 * 3 = 36
Total area of Rect. 2 = 40 * 5 = 200. 
Out of all rectangles, Rectangle 2 has the maximum area.
</span></pre>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Constraints:</strong><br>1 ≤ n ≤ 10<sup>3</sup><br>1 ≤ L, B ≤ 10<sup>4</sup><br>1 ≤ area ≤ 10<sup>8</sup></span></p></div><br><p><span style=font-size:18px><strong>Topic Tags : </strong><br><code>Java-Class and Object</code>&nbsp;
